# Careers-02 — Career Profile & Job Matching

Adds a trainee career profile and deterministic job recommendations to the Careers employment exchange.

## Trainee
- Maintain headline, professional summary, skills, preferred locations and employment types.
- Add a resume/experience summary as text.
- Choose whether the profile is visible to employers.
- Receive recommendations based on skills, experience, education, location and employment-type preferences.

## Employer
- View a candidate career profile from an application.
- Hidden profiles cannot be viewed by ordinary employers; NCCT administrators can still review them.

## Matching
The recommendation score is deterministic, not an AI prediction:
- Skills: up to 60 points
- Experience: up to 20 points
- Education: up to 10 points
- Location preference: up to 5 points
- Employment-type preference: up to 5 points

Only published jobs that the trainee has not already applied to are recommended.
